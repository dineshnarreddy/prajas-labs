1. Who We Are

Prajas Labs Private Limited (CIN: U62099AP2026PTC126173) is an Indian technology company based in Cuddapah, Andhra Pradesh. We build Pinged, a free mobile app that aggregates government job notifications from official sources across India (UPSC, SSC, Railways, Banking, Defence, State PSCs, and more) into a single, searchable feed.

2. How Pinged Works

Pinged does not use accounts, sign-ups, or logins of any kind. It works like a radio broadcast, not a two-way service:

  • Our server continuously collects public government job notifications and downloads the full, current list to your app.
  • Your phone stores your preferences (qualification, state, category, etc.) locally, and uses your phone's own processing power to filter that list down to what's relevant to you.
  • Your preferences are never transmitted to our server. We have no way of knowing what job categories or filters you've set.
In short: the server broadcasts public job data outward; nothing personal comes back.

3. Data We Collect

We do not collect, request, or store any personally identifiable information. Pinged has no registration, no login, no user profile stored on our servers, and no in-app tracking of individual users.

4. Data That Stays On Your Device

To personalize which jobs are shown to you, Pinged stores the following only on your phone, using your device's local storage. This data is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted anywhere:

  • Your selected qualification level, degree/stream, and (if applicable) teaching subject
  • Your selected state and job category preferences
  • Your saved/bookmarked jobs

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data — we hold no copy of it.

5. What Our Server Sees

When Pinged fetches the latest job list, our server receives only standard, anonymous technical request information common to any internet connection (such as your IP address, handled automatically by our hosting infrastructure for routing and abuse prevention). We do not log this against any user identity because no user identity exists in our system.

6. Third-Party Services

Pinged does not integrate any third-party advertising networks, analytics platforms, or social login providers. Job "Apply" links open the official government website or notification PDF directly in your browser — at that point, the respective government website's own privacy policy applies, not ours.

7. Security

All communication between the app and our server is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Because we do not collect personal data, there is no user account database that could be exposed in the event of a security incident.

8. App Permissions

Pinged requests only the minimum permission needed to function: internet access, to download the current job list. It does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, storage, location, or SMS.

9. Children's Privacy

Pinged is intended for job-seeking adults and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13, in line with the fact that the app collects no personal data from any user regardless of age.

10. Your Rights & Choices

Because Pinged stores your preferences only on your device, you are always in control:

  • Change or clear preferences: update them anytime from the app's profile screen.
  • Delete all local data: uninstall the app, or clear its storage/cache from your device's app settings.
  • Access requests: since we hold no personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete on our end.

11. Changes to This Policy

If our data practices ever change — for example, if we introduce optional accounts in the future — we will update this page and revise the "Effective Date" above before the change takes effect in the app.
