About Us

Not Just an AI Company.
A Technology Company
For People.

Prajas Labs is a technology company headquartered in Andhra Pradesh, India. Our founding principle is simple — every product we build must be useful, accessible, and meaningful to real people.

We began with artificial intelligence, but we will not be limited by it. From health technology to financial intelligence, from computer vision to personal AI, we build across every sector where technology can make a genuine difference.

What We Build

Six Domains. One Mission.

Prajas Labs operates across six technology domains — each chosen because it directly improves lives and solves problems that matter.

01

Artificial Intelligence

Custom AI models, deep learning systems, and intelligent automation built for businesses and individuals. Our core capability — applied across every domain.

02

Health Technology

AI-powered diagnostic tools and health monitoring platforms that put the patient first — accessible, private, and built to make a real difference.

03

Financial Intelligence

Intelligent platforms for portfolio management and market intelligence — powerful enough for professionals, simple enough for everyone.

04

Computer Vision

Facial recognition, identity verification, and visual AI systems for security and access control — built on state-of-the-art vision models.

05

Community Platforms

Digital platforms that connect people to their communities — local services, events, and social infrastructure. Technology that brings people together.

06

Personal AI

Voice-enabled intelligent assistants and personal AI tools that work for individuals — private, context-aware, and integrated into everyday life.
