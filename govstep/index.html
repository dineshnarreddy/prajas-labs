How It Works

Built the Opposite Way Round.

Most job sites make you search. GovStep brings the right jobs to you, automatically — nothing to search for, nothing to configure beyond your basic details.

01

We Watch Every Source.

Central and state recruitment boards, railways, banking, defence, SSC, UPSC — checked every day so nothing new is missed.

02

Matched to You Automatically.

Only the jobs that fit your qualification, state, and age — nothing else to filter through yourself.

03

You See What Fits.

Only jobs you can actually apply for — no more scrolling past hundreds of postings for the wrong state or the wrong degree.

04

Save, Apply, Track.

Bookmark anything for later, mark what you have applied to, and every listing links straight to the official government page — never a third-party reseller.

Always the official source

Every "Apply" link on GovStep goes to the recruiting board’s own website or notification PDF — never a third-party reseller, and never a link we’ve invented. If we can’t verify a link is official, we don’t show it.

Privacy First

We Don’t Know Who You Are.

We built GovStep so that using it never means giving up your privacy. No accounts, no data collection — nothing to worry about.

No Accounts, No Login

There is nothing to sign up for. Open the app and your feed is already there — nothing tied to your name, phone number, or email.

Nothing About You Is Collected

We don’t know your name, your qualification, or which jobs you’ve looked at. There is simply nothing stored about who you are.

No Ads. No Tracking.

No advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, nothing watching how you use the app. There is simply nothing to sell.

Delete Anytime

Uninstalling the app removes everything instantly — there is no account to close and nothing left behind.
