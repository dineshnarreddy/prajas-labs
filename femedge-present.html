What We Built

FemEdge AI
Is Already Live.

16+ Health Screenings — All On-Device

PCOS, endometriosis, bone health, mental health, menstrual irregularity, fibroids, thyroid, and more. No internet needed.

Zero Data Leaves the Phone

Fully encrypted local storage. Biometric gate on every launch. GDPR-compliant by design.

Background Passive Screening

As users log daily cycles, symptoms, wearable data, and medications — the AI screens quietly and surfaces high-confidence alerts.

Where It Connects

Clinically
Relevant — by
Design.

Medication Side Effects

Long-term meds cause menstrual disruption and bone loss. FemEdge tracks both passively.

Offline & Rural

Zero connectivity needed. Works on any Android phone in remote areas.

Patient Diaries

Daily symptom logging — same philosophy as a clinical diary, for hormonal health.

Bone Health

Dedicated AI screening for bone density loss markers — relevant to long-term medication patients.
