How It Works

Intelligent. Private. Yours.

WellCycle is not a symptom search engine. It is a clinical AI system trained on validated medical guidelines and real-world datasets — built to give you meaningful, private health intelligence.

01

You Answer. The App Listens.

Guided health questionnaires collect symptoms, cycle patterns, medical history, and lifestyle factors — using clinically-validated question sets.

02

AI Runs on Your Device.

20 specialist AI models run entirely on your phone using ONNX Runtime. Your answers never leave your device for health analysis — not even to us.

03

You Get Clear Insights.

Results highlight risk levels across 20 conditions, explain the contributing factors, and generate a PDF you can share with your doctor.

04

Your Data Stays With You.

All records are encrypted on-device with AES-256. You can delete everything with a single tap. No account is required to run screenings.

Privacy First

Your Health Data Is Never Ours.

WellCycle was designed from day one on a single principle: health data is deeply personal. It belongs to you, lives on your device, and we have no access to it — ever.

On-Device Storage Only

All your health records, cycle data, and screening results are stored in an AES-256 encrypted database on your phone. We have zero access to this data.

AI Runs Locally

Every health screening is run on-device using ONNX Runtime. Your answers never travel to a server for analysis — not to us, not to anyone.

No Ads. No Tracking.

WellCycle contains zero advertising SDKs, zero analytics platforms, and no social login. There is nothing to track and no one to sell your data to.

Delete Anytime

One tap in Settings deletes all your health data permanently. Uninstalling the app has the same effect. You are always in control.
