About Us

Not Just an AI Company.
A Technology Company For People.

Prajas Labs Private Limited is a technology company incorporated in India in 2026. The name Prajas (प्रजास्) means "the people" — and that is our founding principle. Every product we build must be useful, accessible, and meaningful to real people.

We began with artificial intelligence — but we will not be limited by it. From health diagnostics to financial intelligence, from computer vision to community platforms, Prajas Labs is building across sectors wherever technology can make a genuine difference.
