3. Where Your Data Lives

All personal health data in FemEdge AI is stored exclusively in a local SQLite database on your Android device. This database is:

  • Encrypted with AES-256 via SQLCipher — industry-standard full-database encryption
  • Keyed by a secret stored in the Android Keystore — a hardware-backed secure enclave on your device
  • Never automatically backed up to Google Drive, cloud services, or our servers
  • Fully deleted when you uninstall the app or use the "Delete All My Data" option in Settings

On-Device AI Screening: All 20 health screening models (PMOS, Heart Health, GDM, Thyroid, Bone Health, Mental Health, Anaemia, PPD, PMDD, Endometriosis, Breast Cancer, Ovarian Cancer, Menopause, Uterine Cancer, Fibroids, Vaginal Infection, Menstrual Irregularity, Maternal Health, and others) run entirely on your device using ONNX Runtime. Your health data is never sent to a server for AI analysis.

4. What Goes to the Server

Only the following data is transmitted from the app to external servers:

  • AI Chat only: When you use the FemEdge AI chat assistant, your typed message is sent to a Google Gemini AI endpoint (via our Railway API relay) to generate a response. Chat messages are processed in transit and not stored by Prajas Labs.
  • Authentication tokens: A JWT (JSON Web Token) is issued on login and used to authenticate API requests. No health data is included in authentication calls.
  • Force-update config: The app periodically checks a minimum version number from our API to enforce critical security updates. No personal data is sent in this request.
  • PDF generation (optional): If you choose to export a health summary PDF, a report is generated on-device. No data is uploaded to generate the PDF.

We never send health screening results, symptom inputs, cycle data, mood logs, or personal medical history to any server.

6. Third-Party Services

FemEdge AI uses the following third-party services in limited scope:

  • Google Gemini API (via Railway relay) — processes AI chat messages only. Google's privacy policy applies to messages in transit. We do not send health data to this service.
  • Railway.app — hosts our backend API for authentication and AI chat relay. No health data is stored on Railway servers.
  • Google Play Services — standard Android services for app distribution, crash reporting (if enabled by the OS), and Health Connect integration (if you grant permission).
  • Android Health Connect (optional) — if you grant permission, FemEdge AI reads steps, heart rate, sleep, and SpO₂ from Health Connect. This data is processed on-device and never transmitted to our servers.

We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics platforms, social login, or any third-party tracking. There are no ads in FemEdge AI.

7. Your Rights

If you are a user in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with comprehensive data protection laws, you have the following rights:

Access Correction Erasure Portability Restriction Objection

Because almost all data is stored locally on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly within the app:

  • Access & Export: Use the PDF export feature to access all your health data in a readable format.
  • Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Go to Settings → Delete Account & Data to permanently delete all local data. Uninstalling the app also removes all data.
  • Portability: Export your data as a PDF from within the app at any time.
  • Correction: Edit any symptom entry, cycle record, or profile field directly in the app.

To exercise rights related to server-side account data (login credentials, JWT tokens), email us at contact@prajaslabs.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In the UK: Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). In the EU: your national data protection authority.

8. Children's Privacy

FemEdge AI is designed for adult women (18 years and older). We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18. If you believe a child has provided health information through the app, please contact us at contact@prajaslabs.com and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app, legal requirements, or our practices. When we make significant changes:

Continued use of FemEdge AI after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
