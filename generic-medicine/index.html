How It Works

A Store Finder That Works Anywhere.

No account, no waiting on a server. The store list is already on your phone, so finding the nearest one is instant — even with no signal.

01

The Whole List Ships With the App.

Every mapped Jan Aushadhi store across India is built into the app, so it is ready the moment you install it.

02

Search Near Me, or by Pincode.

Tap once to use your location, or type any 6-digit pincode. Either way the search runs on your phone — no connection required.

03

Nearest First, With Distance.

Stores are sorted by how far they are, so the closest one is always at the top, with its address and contact details.

04

Directions and Call, One Tap.

Open a store in your maps app or ring it directly. Links always hand off to your own maps and dialer — nothing in between.

Independent — not a government app

Generic Medicine Finder is an independent app by Prajas Labs. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Government of India, the Pharmaceuticals & Medical Devices Bureau of India (PMBI), or the Jan Aushadhi (PMBJP) programme. Store information is drawn from public open data, so please confirm details with the store before travelling.

Privacy First

Find a Store Without Being Tracked.

We built Generic Medicine Finder so that using it never means giving up your privacy. No accounts, no data collection — nothing to worry about.

No Accounts, No Login

There is nothing to sign up for. Open the app and start searching — nothing is tied to your name, phone number, or email.

Your Location Stays on Your Phone

If you search “near me”, your location is used only on your device to sort nearby stores, and is never sent to us or anyone else.

No Ads. No Tracking.

No advertising networks, no analytics, nothing watching how you use the app. There is simply nothing to sell.

Delete Anytime

Uninstalling the app removes everything instantly — there is no account to close and nothing left behind.
