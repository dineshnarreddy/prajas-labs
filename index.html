Not just an AI company. A technology company for people.

Prajas Labs is a technology company headquartered in Andhra Pradesh, India. Our founding principle is simple — every product we build must be useful, accessible, and meaningful to real people.

We began with artificial intelligence, but we will not be limited by it. From health technology to financial intelligence, from computer vision to personal AI, we build across every sector where technology can make a genuine difference.
