Practice Test Sources

GovStep is an independent, privately developed app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any government, examination body or recruiting organisation. The practice questions are GovStep's own and are not official examination papers; a practice score does not predict any official result.

This page lists every exam-conducting body behind the practice tests currently available in the app. It is generated from the app's own catalogue and refreshes automatically. List as of 2026-10-05.
