How It Works

Intelligent. Private. Yours.

WellCycle is not a search engine for symptoms. It is built with trusted medical knowledge to give you meaningful, private answers about your own health — in words you can actually understand.

01

You Answer a Few Questions.

Simple, caring questions about how you feel, your cycle, and your health history. No medical knowledge needed — just answer honestly.

02

Your Phone Does the Thinking.

All the intelligence lives inside your phone. Your answers are understood right there — nothing is sent to the internet, not even to us.

03

You Get Clear Insights.

Easy-to-understand insights highlight patterns that may be worth discussing with your doctor — plus a simple report you can bring along. WellCycle informs; only a doctor can diagnose.

04

Your Information Stays Yours.

Everything is locked away safely on your phone. One tap deletes it all, anytime. You never even need to create an account.

A companion, not a doctor

WellCycle is a wellness and health-awareness app. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Its insights are there to help you have better, earlier conversations with a qualified healthcare professional — always consult your doctor about any health concern. If you are experiencing an emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.

Privacy First

Your Health Data Is Never Ours.

WellCycle was designed from day one on a single principle: health data is deeply personal. It belongs to you, lives on your device, and we have no access to it — ever.

Everything Stays on Your Phone

Your health records, cycle notes, and results live only on your phone — locked and protected. We cannot see them. Nobody can.

No Internet Needed for Checks

Your health checks happen right on your phone. Your answers never travel to a server — not to us, not to anyone.

No Ads. No Tracking.

There are no advertisements and nothing watching what you do. There is simply nothing to track and no one to sell your information to.

Delete Anytime

One tap in Settings removes all your health information permanently. Uninstalling the app does the same. You are always in control.
